✦ WHO WE ARE

Genius ZOO is Chattogram’s first dedicated STEM education center, born from over a decade of vision and a belief that every child deserves world-class science education.

We bring physics, mathematics, technology and engineering together in one living, hands-on experience—guided by expert educators and an original curriculum inspired by Oxford-standard teaching.
